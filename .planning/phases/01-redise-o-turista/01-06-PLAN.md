<objective>
Restyle the Turista map surfaces and the emergency screen onto the warm token system, without letting
a warm palette cost a map overlay its contrast or an alarm screen its urgency.

Purpose: these three screens are where "make it warmer" and "keep it functional" pull hardest against
each other. Maps demand high-contrast overlays; an emergency screen demands alarm. Both constraints
outrank the palette.

Output: three restyled screens, with the activity-status vocabulary unified across the app.
</objective>
